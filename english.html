Begin by naming your character and choosing a knight, warrior, mage, or archer. Each class has different talents. Distribute 20 points among strength, agility, intelligence, and health: mages may favor intelligence for mana, while archers may favor agility for accuracy and evasion. Choose one of five weapon specialties, then customize your character’s gender, hairstyle, hair color, and skin tone before beginning the adventure.
Four kingdoms span five continents and more than 30 towns. Each continent may take 20 hours to complete, with puzzles, traps, and treasures to discover. Meet many characters and complete quests in different ways. Your choices and moral alignment influence the story.
Good and evil coexist in Dispel. Use 41 spells strategically, choosing the power of each cast; stronger magic takes longer to charge. Weapons, armor, shoes, helmets, and treasures can improve combat, defense, or mana. Manage limited inventory space and adapt your equipment to each dungeon and enemy.
Monsters move independently rather than waiting in one place. Retreat and train if enemies overwhelm you. Companions join your adventure and respond to combat, fighting alongside you as allies.
Day gives way to night, and rain or snow changes the weather. Dispel features 3D lighting and reflections; characters change appearance with their armor, with detailed textures bringing them to life.
